MON ALPHABET EN FAMILLE — ALL COLLECTIONS AND CONTINUOUS PLAYLISTS

LAPTOP TEST (no public hosting needed)
1. Extract this ZIP into a normal folder.
2. On Windows, double-click start-laptop.cmd. Python 3 must be installed.
   Or open a terminal in the folder and run:
     Windows:  py -3 start-laptop.py
     macOS/Linux: python3 start-laptop.py
3. Open http://localhost:8000 in Chrome on that same laptop.
   Keep using this same address and port to retain the same browser storage.
4. Choose Parents > Voix et videos > Video avec voix > Ouvrir la camera.
   Allow microphone and camera access. Preview, then Demarrer la video.
5. Stop, preview your clip, and choose Conserver ce clip.

SIX TABS
Lettres keeps the 26-letter poster. Syllabes starts with 20 syllables, and Mots
with 12 illustrated words. Nombres starts with 0 to 20. Noms propres is ready
for your family's names, places and photos. Tout combines all five collections.
Use Tout > Tout lire to watch videos across every category. Original words shared
between Lettres and Mots, and videos assigned to multiple people, play only once.

Parents > Contenu lets you add or edit words, syllables, numbers, proper nouns
and pictures. Tout updates automatically; content is edited in its original category.
For Lettres, select the letter, then Nouveau mot or an existing word.
Attach up to 12 pictures per entry; images are optional. Select Enregistrer
les modifications to save, or Enregistrer sa voix ou video to open the studio.
The original letter illustrations stay available alongside your additions.
New syllables, numbers, proper nouns and custom words need a family recording
for pronunciation. Numbers and proper nouns have separate, stable recording keys.

WORD MEDIA AND PEOPLE
Open any word and choose + Image, + Voix or + Video to add media directly.
The studio opens on the matching word. Start the microphone or camera explicitly.
Each word can keep several voice clips and video takes. Nouveau clip adds a take
without replacing the previous ones. Select a saved clip and check its people
under Qui parle ou apparait ? Changes save automatically. A clip can involve
several family members and appears in each person's video filter.

On the main screen, Videos avec lets you check several people. Only content with
a video involving at least one checked person is shown. Toute la famille clears
the selection. A shared video is included once. Tout lire plays the matching clips
from the current category consecutively; the Tout tab includes all categories.
Choose Videos or Son seul; with people selected, Son seul plays only the qualifying
videos' soundtracks. With no selection, Son seul includes saved voices and videos.
Pause/Continuer, Precedent, Suivant and Arreter control the sequence. Closing the
word keeps it playing through the small playback bar. You can browse tabs and
pages without losing the list. Touching a recorded word jumps to it in the list;
a new recorded word outside that list is inserted next, and the rest continues.
A card without a suitable clip leaves the current video playing. Revoir or the
image replays the current clip and preserves the rest of the list. Arreter stops it.

In the word view, use Plein ecran beside Revoir. This player keeps the image at
the top left and the word, playlist and transport controls on screen. The same
video element and list continue through fullscreen transitions. Reduire returns
to the word view. If the browser does not offer native fullscreen, the player
fills the page instead. A hidden/background tab pauses playback; Continuer may
restart the interrupted clip when you return.

Several audio clips and videos can be kept for each entry.
The video plays when a letter is opened. Parents > Affichage > Videos de famille
switches to sound only. In the poster's listen-only mode, only sound is played.
Direct recordings stop after 30 seconds. Imported videos can be up to 60 seconds
and 25 MB; the browser must support the codec. MP4 and WebM are suggested.
You can record in another app and use Importer une video if device access fails.

The localhost server listens on this laptop only. Its address does not let a
tablet connect to the laptop. Use an HTTPS host when you want tablet access.
Opening index.html directly still supports playback and clip import, but PWA
installation and direct microphone/camera access require an appropriate origin.

OFFLINE USE
Wait for Pret hors ligne before disconnecting. Install from Chrome's menu if you
want an application shortcut. The cached app and locally stored clips work offline.
If this is an update to a previous installation, choose Parents > Sauvegarde >
Mettre a jour l'alphabet. Finish or discard any pending recording first.

FAMILY DATA AND BACKUPS
Photos, audio and videos stay in this browser on this device; the application does
not upload them. They do not automatically follow you to a different URL or device.
Use Parents > Sauvegarde > Sauvegarder le contenu et les clips, then Importer une sauvegarde at
the destination. Earlier v1, v2, v3 and v4 JSON backups remain supported. New v5 backups include
all custom content, pictures, audio, videos and shared recorder assignments. Import replaces the current family collection after confirmation.
Old backups automatically gain the new number and proper-noun sections. Existing
clips, pictures, profiles and assignments are retained. Explicit empty sections
stay empty. Do not import a v5 backup into an older app version.
Exporter mon alphabet creates a standalone HTML copy containing the entire family
collection. Videos make these exported files larger. Keep backups outside the browser.

LATER: GITHUB PAGES OR ANOTHER STATIC HTTPS HOST
The ready-to-host application files are:
  index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icon.svg, icon-192.png, icon-512.png,
  and .nojekyll (included in this ZIP, sometimes hidden by the file manager).
No build, application server, account system, or backend database is required.

For GitHub Pages, place those files at your repository's publishing root, then
choose that branch and root folder under Settings > Pages. Use the HTTPS URL
GitHub provides. All application paths are relative, including service-worker scope,
so a project URL such as https://USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY/ is supported.
Do not upload a personalized HTML export or a family backup to a public repository.
Use the blank index.html from this ZIP and import family clips privately in the app.

Changing host changes the browser storage origin. Export first, then import at the
new address. Keep the old copy until you have checked the imported collection.

Permissions help in the parent studio reports detected secure-context or hosting
policy restrictions. It cannot override Chrome, operating-system, or hosting policy.
